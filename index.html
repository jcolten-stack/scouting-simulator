How to read the board

OVR is the average of the six MLT-graded ratings (40 to 99), scored when the student applies as a sophomore. Hover any column header to see what it measures.

Wind turns the opportunity-to-date index into sprint terms. A headwind (−) means the student had less access to opportunity. It's shown for interpretation only.

+/− Exp is "distance traveled": how far a student's OVR sits above or below what students with similar opportunity typically score.

Growth trait comes from the student's real first-year record (fall to spring GPA change and credits earned), not from a predicted ceiling, so family background can't set a student's "potential."

Fit combines skill-fit (major-course GPA vs. overall GPA, plus self-rated ability) with interest-fit (how well the student’s Career Personality Profiler interest areas match the declared or intended major).

Track is the student's primary career track, one of six: Finance, Consulting, Corporate Management, Software Engineering / Tech, Healthcare, and Public Relations, Media & Entertainment. It's drawn only from tracks that fit their major and is ranked by how well their Career Personality Profiler interest areas match each track. To target shows how far they are, on average, from MLT's benchmark for that track by junior-summer internship recruiting.
